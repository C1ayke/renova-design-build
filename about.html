Our Story

Family integrity. Building science. Red Seal mastery.

Renova Design Build is a Surrey, BC-based general contractor and Red Seal carpentry firm run by the Quesnel family, serving the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland — a business built on the same commitment since day one: the person who quotes the job is the person standing on-site running it. That family reputation has grown over decades of hands-on work — structural concrete on commercial job sites, an equestrian arena in Langley, custom homes in West Vancouver and on the Sunshine Coast, and full renovations in Surrey and New Westminster.

For the Quesnels, that reputation shows up as much in the work you don't see — the formwork bracing, the structural connections, the code compliance done right the first time — as it does in the finished cedar siding or the custom staircase. The business has grown almost entirely on word-of-mouth and repeat referral work, built on a reputation for detailed craftsmanship paired with the practical experience to run a job start to finish. Each project is treated as part of an ongoing family tradition, not just another job on the books.

What sets us apart is the pairing of building science with trade mastery. Bill Sr.'s decades as a Journeyman Building Technologist mean every project is grounded in structural code compliance from day one — not retrofitted after a problem shows up. Bill Jr. and Nicholas bring that technical foundation to the ground, as Red Seal Journeyman Carpenters who lead crews directly rather than delegate to unsupervised sub-trades. The result is a firm that pairs masterful specialty carpentry with the full general-contracting scope — design-build, permitting, budgeting, scheduling, and multi-trade coordination — and can move fluidly between a full residential renovation and a multi-month commercial concrete formwork contract without losing the quality control that comes from direct, on-site trade-leader supervision.

Today Renova works both as a primary design-build contractor for custom homes and community facilities, and as a high-precision subcontractor for large commercial developers — a dual capability built on the same crew, the same standards, and the same family name. Sustainable, resource-conscious building practices are part of that standard too — the Quesnels build with an eye toward material efficiency and long-term environmental responsibility on every project.

Leadership

The trade leaders on your job site.

Renova is led by three Quesnel-family trade leaders. There's no separate management layer between you and the crew — the people below quote the project and run it on-site.

Bill Sr.

Journeyman Building Technologist

Structural and technical acumen across building science, structural code compliance, and turnkey general contracting. Bill Sr. also manages capital construction programs for remote community clients, overseeing multi-property scopes from planning through completion.

Bill Jr.

Red Seal Journeyman Carpenter, Foreman

In construction since he was 18, Bill Jr. brings formwork and framing acumen honed on large-scale structural concrete jobs. He manages site-safety compliance and directs crews on custom residential remodels and commercial concrete contracts.

Nicholas

Red Seal Journeyman Carpenter, Superintendent

Architectural and finish acumen spanning fine finish millwork, mitered siding and soffit detailing, and 3D CAD/SketchUp drafting. Nicholas superintends builds and translates client vision into buildable, structurally sound drawings.
