Custom Home

Cove Beach

Sechelt, BC · Waterfront custom home · Lead carpentry subcontractor for West Coast Turn Key

A modern glass-and-timber waterfront home set into a rock bluff above the ocean. Renova delivered the mitered cedar rainscreen siding with true waterfall corners and the hemlock waterfall soffits inside and out. At the main entryway, the crew ran the hemlock soffit as one continuous line from the interior ceiling straight through to the exterior overhang with no visible break at the glass — one of the more impressive details of the job.

  • Scope: Mitered cedar siding, hemlock soffit ceilings
  • Client: West Coast Turn Key

Equestrian & Agricultural

Coast Performance Horses

Langley, BC · Riding arena, barn & post replacement

Renova's primary scope on this property was replacing roughly 90 rotten support posts with new pressure-treated posts — structural remediation that kept a working equestrian facility safe without shutting it down. The project also included rebuilding the arena's interior kick wall, plus a new riding arena and barn with a standing-seam metal roof and three framed lantern-roof cupolas built on-site and craned into place.

  • Scope: Post replacement (~90 posts), kick wall reconstruction, structural framing, lantern-roof cupolas, cladding, doors, interior finishing
