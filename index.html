What We Do

Full general contracting, led by master carpenters.

Renova runs the complete general-contracting scope — design-build, permitting, project management, budgeting, and multi-trade coordination — with the same crew that self-performs the structural concrete, framing, and finish carpentry. From first sketch to final trim, every discipline below is led on-site by a Red Seal trade leader, not handed off to unsupervised sub-crews.

Our Process

From first conversation to final walkthrough.

Every project follows the same real customer journey, whether it's a full custom home or a single-room renovation.

01

Vision

We start by listening — understanding how you want to live in the space and what the project needs to achieve.

02

Collaboration

Your ideas and our trade experience come together early, before anything is locked in on paper.

03

Concept

Layout, massing, and material direction take shape as an early concept for you to react to.

04

Design

The concept becomes detailed, engineer-stamped drawings — in-house 3D modeling keeps every decision visual.

05

Planning

Permitting, scheduling, and budgeting are locked down before a single crew shows up on-site.

06

Execution

Our own crews build it, led on-site every day by a Red Seal trade leader — not handed off to sub-crews.

07

Finishing

Trim, millwork, and the fine details that separate a good build from a photographed one.

08

Cleaning

The site is cleaned and prepped for a final walkthrough — ready to move in, not just ready to leave.

09

Goodbyes

Final handover and walkthrough — and, for most clients, the start of a relationship we hear from again.
